# Lume

A small, free photo editor for JPG and PNG — a personal project by [Giacomo Bozzato](https://giacomobozzato.com).

**Download:** see [Releases](https://github.com/giacomo-bozzato/lume/releases/latest)
- `Lume-mac-arm64.dmg` — Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer)
- `Lume-mac-x64.dmg` — Intel Mac
- `Lume-windows.zip` — Windows, experimental

### Opening it the first time
A quick note from me: the first time you open Lume, your Mac will say it can't verify the developer.
Don't worry, it's all fine — I just don't pay for Apple's certificate, since Lume is a free side project.
Open Lume, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. Only once.

On Windows, if "Windows protected your PC" appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.

No account, no tracking: your photos never leave your computer.
